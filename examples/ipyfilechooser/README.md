## ipyfilechooser app

<a href='https://connect.posit.cloud/publish?framework=shiny&sourceRepositoryURL=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fposit-dev%2Fpy-shinywidgets&sourceRef=main&sourceRefType=branch&primaryFile=examples%2Fipyfilechooser%2Fapp.py&pythonVersion=3.11'><img src='https://cdn.connect.posit.cloud/assets/deploy-to-connect-blue.svg' align="right" /></a>

What `@render_ipyfilechooser` adds over a file-upload input: users can browse and select files that are already on the **server** (no upload needed), sandboxed to a directory you choose, with glob filtering — and the selection feeds straight back into your reactive graph.
